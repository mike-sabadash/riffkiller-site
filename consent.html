Version 2026-10-29

By ticking the box when creating an account, or by continuing with Google, Yandex or Patreon, I freely, specifically, knowingly and unambiguously give my consent to the operator of Riff Killer (riffkiller.fun), an individual registered in the Russian Federation as a self-employed professional, contact support@riffkiller.fun, to process my personal data on the following terms.

  • Data: email, name, profile picture, account ID of the sign-in service, password hash, practice progress, favorites, language, time zone, payment and subscription details, messages to support, IP address and browser data.
  • Purposes: creating and running my account; giving access to free and premium content; saving my progress; accepting payments, renewals and refunds and issuing receipts; sending service emails and, if I turn it on, the weekly practice summary; answering my requests; keeping the Service secure.
  • Actions: collection, recording, organisation, storage, updating, use, transfer to the processors named in the Privacy Policy, blocking, deletion and destruction, with automated means.
  • Cross-border transfer: I agree that my data is stored on a server outside Russia (in the European Union) and transferred to the services named in the Privacy Policy, including in countries that may not provide the same level of protection as my own.
  • Term and withdrawal: the consent is valid until my account is deleted or until I withdraw it. I can withdraw it at any time by deleting my account in the profile or by writing to support@riffkiller.fun. After withdrawal, data is deleted within 30 days, except data the operator must keep by law (payment records).
