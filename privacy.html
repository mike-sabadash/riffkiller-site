Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-29 · Effective 29 October 2026

In short: we collect only what the Service needs to work: your account, your practice progress and your payments. We do not sell data, show ads or use analytics or tracking cookies. You can delete your account at any time in your profile.

1. Who is responsible

The controller (operator) of your personal data is the individual who runs Riff Killer, registered in the Russian Federation as a self-employed professional (see the Terms). Contact for any privacy question or request: support@riffkiller.fun.

3. Cookies and local storage

We use one cookie: the session cookie that keeps you signed in. Your browser's local storage keeps your language, progress, favorites and a few interface settings (for example, whether you have already seen the practice tour), so the site works the same on your next visit. All of this is strictly necessary for the features you use. There are no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking cookies, so we do not show a cookie consent banner. Fonts are served from our own server.

You can clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings at any time; you will be signed out and unsynced progress on that device will be lost.

4. Who else processes the data

We share data only with services that help run Riff Killer, and only what each one needs:

  • Hosting: our server, located outside Russia in the European Union, stores the site and all account data.
  • Resend (USA): sends our emails (your email address and the message).
  • Google, Yandex, Patreon: only if you choose to sign in with them; they tell us your name, email, picture and account ID. Patreon also tells us your membership status.
  • lava.top: processes card payments and renewals; receives the email for the receipt and the order details.
  • TRON blockchain: USDT transfers are public by nature; we only match the transaction ID to your order.

Some of these services are outside your country, including outside the EU and outside Russia. Where GDPR applies, transfers rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses offered by these providers. We do not sell or rent personal data, and we disclose it to authorities only where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and progress: while your account exists. When you delete it, we erase it at once; copies in backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Payment records: for as long as tax law requires (in Russia, up to 5 years), then deleted.
  • Server logs: about 14 days.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after the conversation ends.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, or send it to you in a portable format. You can withdraw your consent at any time (for the weekly summary, with one click in any summary email or in your profile). You can delete your account yourself in the profile. For anything else, write to support@riffkiller.fun; we answer within 30 days (within 10 working days for requests under Russian law). You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority in your country.

7. Security

Data travels over HTTPS, passwords are stored only as hashes, access to the server is limited to the operator, and payment card details never reach our server.

8. Children

The Service is not meant for children under 16 without the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us data without such consent, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We will update this policy when our processing changes. The version and date are at the top; for important changes we will tell signed-in users by email or on the site.

3. Cookie и локальное хранилище

Мы используем одну cookie — сессионную, чтобы ты оставался в аккаунте. В локальном хранилище браузера хранятся язык, прогресс, избранное и несколько настроек интерфейса (например, видел ли ты обучение на странице практики). Всё это строго необходимо для работы функций, которыми ты пользуешься. Аналитических, рекламных и сторонних следящих cookie нет, поэтому мы не показываем баннер согласия на cookie. Шрифты загружаются с нашего сервера.

Очистить cookie и локальное хранилище можно в настройках браузера; при этом ты выйдешь из аккаунта, а несинхронизированный прогресс на этом устройстве пропадёт.

4. Кто ещё обрабатывает данные

  • Хостинг: наш сервер находится за пределами России, в Европейском союзе; на нём хранятся сайт и данные аккаунтов.
  • Resend (США): отправка писем (email и текст письма).
  • Google, Яндекс, Patreon: только если ты входишь через них; они передают имя, email, фото и ID аккаунта. Patreon также передаёт статус членства.
  • lava.top: приём оплаты картой и продлений; получает email для чека и данные заказа.
  • Блокчейн TRON: переводы USDT публичны по своей природе; мы только сопоставляем ID транзакции с заказом.

Часть этих сервисов находится за пределами России и ЕС, поэтому происходит трансграничная передача данных; ты даёшь на неё согласие (см. Согласие). Мы не продаём и не сдаём данные в аренду и передаём их государственным органам только в случаях, предусмотренных законом.

5. Сроки хранения

  • Аккаунт и прогресс — пока существует аккаунт. При удалении мы стираем их сразу; копии в резервных копиях перезаписываются в течение 30 дней.

  • Журналы сервера — около 14 дней.
  • Переписка с поддержкой — до 2 лет после её окончания.
